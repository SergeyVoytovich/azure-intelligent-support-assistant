# SVoy Electronics GmbH

SVoy Electronics GmbH is a fictional German online retailer specializing in consumer electronics, computer accessories and smart-home devices.

## Customer Support

Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 CET.

Customers can contact support for questions about:

- orders
- shipping
- returns
- refunds
- warranty
- payments
- customer accounts

Complex cases can be escalated to a human support agent.
