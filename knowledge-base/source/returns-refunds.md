# Returns and Refunds

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days after delivery.

Products should be returned complete and, where reasonably possible, in their original packaging.

## Return Shipping

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly delivered products.

For other returns, the customer normally pays the return shipping costs.

## Refunds

Refunds are normally processed within 5 business days after the returned product has been inspected.

The refund is sent to the original payment method.

If a refund has not arrived within 10 business days after confirmation, the case should be escalated to customer support.
