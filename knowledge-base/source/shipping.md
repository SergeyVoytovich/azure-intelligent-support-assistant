# Shipping Policy

SVoy Electronics currently ships to addresses within Germany.

## Standard Shipping

Standard delivery normally takes 2–4 business days.

Orders with a value of at least EUR 50 qualify for free standard shipping.

For orders below EUR 50, standard shipping costs EUR 4.99.

## Express Shipping

Express shipping costs EUR 9.99.

Orders placed before 12:00 CET on a business day are normally delivered on the next business day.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent by email after the order has been handed over to the shipping provider.

If tracking information has not changed for more than 5 business days, the customer should contact support.
